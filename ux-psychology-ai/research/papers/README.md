## Key Papers by Topic

### Visual Perception (Gestalt)
- Wertheimer (1923): Foundational Gestalt grouping paper. Original in German. English translation available in various collections. Primary source for Proximity, Similarity, Closure, Continuity, Common Fate, Prägnanz.

### Working Memory
- Miller (1956): The famous "7±2" paper. Important: read the original. It concerns information transmission limits, not a design rule for menu items. Often misrepresented.
- Cowan (2001): Modern working memory capacity estimate (3–4 chunks). More relevant to contemporary interface design than Miller's 1956 figure.

### Motor Behavior
- Fitts (1954): Original Fitts's Law paper. Mathematically rigorous. The Shannon formulation (used in HCI) is from MacKenzie (1992).

### Decision and Judgment
- Tversky & Kahneman (1974): Heuristics and biases paper. Anchoring, representativeness, availability. Classic.
- Kahneman & Tversky (1979): Prospect theory. Loss aversion and framing effects with full mathematical model.

### Attention
- Simons & Levin (1997): Change blindness review. Accessible and comprehensive.
- Pirolli & Card (1999): Information foraging theory. The theoretical basis for information scent.
