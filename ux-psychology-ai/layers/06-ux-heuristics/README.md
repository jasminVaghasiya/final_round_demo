# Layer 06 — UX Heuristics and Design Principles

**Purpose:** Provide established usability evaluation heuristics and widely-used UX principles for evaluating and designing interfaces.

---

## Scope

This layer contains:
- Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics (the most widely used heuristic evaluation framework)
- Additional UX principles with varying evidence levels

**Important classification note:** Heuristics are not empirical laws. They are expert-derived evaluation criteria. Nielsen's heuristics emerged from factor analysis of usability problems and expert review (Molich & Nielsen, 1990; Nielsen & Molich, 1990). They have strong practical support but are distinct from experimentally derived laws like Fitts's Law.
