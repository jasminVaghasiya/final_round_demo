# Layer 03 — Decision Psychology

**Purpose:** Explain how users make choices, respond to options, and are influenced by how choices are presented.

---

## Scope

This layer covers behavioral and cognitive research on human decision-making, with direct application to interface choice architecture, default design, sequencing, and motivational design.

**What is not in this layer:**
- Cognitive processing before decisions form → Layer 02
- How attention is captured by choice-relevant elements → Layer 04
- The mechanics of operating controls → Layer 05

---

## Important Evidence Note

Decision psychology research is mature but complex. Several principles in this layer have:
- Been established in controlled laboratory conditions
- Been extended to consumer contexts through field studies
- Shown context-dependent effects that do not always replicate in all settings

Every principle in this layer is classified with its actual evidence level. **Do not treat all decision effects as universal laws.** They are behavioral tendencies with boundary conditions.
